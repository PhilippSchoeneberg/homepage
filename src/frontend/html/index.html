About me

Hello! I'm Philipp. I study computer science with a focus on artificial intelligence as a dual student, combining lectures at DHBW Mannheim with practical phases at the DLR Institute of Space Systems in Bremen.

During my school years at the Gymnasium Athenaeum in Stade, where I completed my Abitur in 2024, I spent a lot of time on research. Some of my projects took part in Jugend forscht, others I presented as talks, posters and articles at the German Physical Society (DPG). You can find them on this website, together with some projects I did in private.

In my free time I enjoy meeting up with friends, doing sports – the gym, running, swimming and bouldering – and thinking about the world and the cosmos.
