2. Hosting on GitHub Pages

3. No cookies and no tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use any analytics or tracking services and does not load content such as fonts or scripts from third parties.

4. Storing your display preference

If you switch between the light and dark theme with the button in the header, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so that it is kept for your next visit. This information does not leave your device and is not sent to me. It is only stored when you press the button yourself (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

5. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process your details in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed for this purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply.

6. Links to other websites

This website contains links to external sites, for example GitHub, the German Physical Society or Scratch. Data is only transferred to the respective provider once you follow such a link; their privacy policy applies from then on.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). Just send me an email.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.
