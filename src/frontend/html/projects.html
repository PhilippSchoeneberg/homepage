Stadle

  • Web application
  • School seminar

In the “Public History” seminar at school, my classmates and I developed a web application that introduces the residents of the historic town of Stade to the history of their home town. Inspired by GeoGuessr and Wordle, players search for a historical object using pictures and historical facts – with one quiz per day to keep the interest high. We hope it encourages young people in particular to learn more about their town's history and makes learning history more fun.
